Why does my child keep getting colds and coughs?

Your questions, answered simply. Each section starts with a parent's question.

1

Why is my child getting colds and coughs so often, when other children are not?

Sensitive airways can look normal from outside, but react faster.

Some children's airways (the tubes that carry air to the lungs) are more sensitive than others. Some are born that way. In others, many viral infections over time leave the airways sore and easily upset. Even when your child looks well, these airways are already on alert.

When a cold or viral fever reaches them, the airways swell and make extra mucus. The narrowed tube makes a whistling sound and the cough begins. Your child is not weak. Their airways simply react more strongly.

Mera bachcha hi baar baar cold aur khaansi se kyon pareshan hota hai, jabki aur bachche nahi hote?

Kuch bachchon ki saans ki naliyan (airways) doosron se zyada sensitive hoti hain. Kuch ko yeh janam se milta hai. Kuch mein kai viral infections ke baad airways kachche aur jaldi bhadakne waale ho jaate hain. Bachcha theek dikhe, tab bhi yeh airways alert par rehte hain.

Jab cold ya viral fever inhe chhoota hai, toh airways sooj jaate hain aur extra mucus banta hai. Sankri nali se seeti jaisi awaaz aati hai aur khaansi shuru ho jaati hai. Bachcha kamzor nahi hai, bas airways thoda zyada react karte hain.

2

Why do some children start coughing when crying, laughing, running or jumping?

Fast, deep breathing can start a cough only in some children.

Only some children do this. When a child runs, jumps, cries hard or laughs, they breathe faster and deeper. Air rushes through a sensitive airway, and the cough can start, even without a cold that day. It is a sign that the airways are sensitive.

Not every child with wheeze has this symptom. If your child does not cough while playing, that does not mean an inhaler is not needed. The decision depends on the whole picture: how often the cough and cold return, night cough, and how the airways respond. Your doctor decides after examining your child.

Kuch bachche rone, hasne, daudne ya koodne par khaansi kyon karte hain?

Sirf kuch bachchon mein yeh hota hai. Jab bachcha daudta hai, koodta hai, zor se rota hai ya hasta hai, toh saans tez aur gehri chalti hai. Hawa sensitive airway se zor se guzarti hai, aur bina cold ke bhi khaansi shuru ho sakti hai. Yeh sanket hai ki airways sensitive hain.

Har wheeze wale bachche mein yeh lakshan nahi hota. Agar aapka bachcha khelte time khaansi nahi karta, toh iska matlab yeh nahi ki inhaler ki zaroorat nahi. Faisla poori tasveer par hota hai: cold aur khaansi kitni baar aate hain, raat ki khaansi, aur airways kaise react karte hain. Doctor bachche ko jaanch kar faisla karte hain.

3

Why does my child cough more at night, a day or two after the cold?

At night, the airway is narrower and mucus collects.

Swelling and mucus build up over the first day or two of a cold, so the cough often gets worse after that. At night, mucus collects when your child is lying down, and the airways narrow a little. The cough can wake your child.

Cold ke ek-do din baad raat mein khaansi zyada kyon hoti hai?

Cold ke pehle ek-do din mein sooj aur mucus badhta hai, isliye khaansi baad mein badh jaati hai. Raat mein bachcha lete hai toh mucus jama hota hai aur airways thode sankre ho jaate hain. Khaansi se bachcha jaag bhi sakta hai.

4

Why does the cough keep coming back, even after the medicine worked?

Medicine calms the symptoms. The hidden swelling stays, and the next cold restarts the cycle.

Short medicine clears the mucus and calms the swelling for a while, so your child feels better. But the hidden swelling inside the airway can still be there. When the next cold or viral fever comes, the airway reacts again. That is why a regular controller medicine is often needed.

Dawa se theek hone ke baad bhi khaansi wapas kyon aati hai?

Kuch din ki dawa mucus aur sooj ko thodi der ke liye kam karti hai, isliye bachcha theek lagta hai. Lekin airway ke andar chhupi hui sooj baaki reh sakti hai. Agla cold ya viral fever aaye toh airway phir react karta hai. Isliye roz ki controller dawa aksar zaroori hoti hai.

5

Will my child always have this? Will they outgrow it?

Many children have fewer and milder attacks as their airways grow bigger. This is not certain for every child. What we can do now is protect the airways, so your child can play, sleep and go to school normally.

Kya bachche ko hamesha yeh rahega? Kya badhte umar mein theek ho jaayega?

Kai bachchon mein umar ke saath airways bade hone par attacks kam aur halke ho jaate hain. Yeh har bachche ke liye pakka nahi hai. Abhi hum airways ko bachaate hain, taaki bachcha khel sake, so sake aur school ja sake.

6

Why does the doctor say my child needs an inhaler?

An inhaler sends the medicine straight to the airways, where the swelling is. The controller inhaler calms the swelling over days to weeks, so it is used every day, even on good days. The reliever opens tight muscles within minutes, when a cough or wheeze is present.

Doctor ne inhaler kyon bataya hai?

Inhaler dawa seedha airways tak pahunchata hai, jahan sooj hai. Controller inhaler sooj ko kuch din se hafte bhar mein shaant karta hai, isliye bachche ke theek hone par bhi roz dena zaroori hai. Reliever tight muscles ko minutes mein khol deta hai, jab khaansi ya wheeze ho.

7

Why not give a syrup instead?

Inhaler: mostly to the lungs. Syrup and tablet: through the whole body.

Syrups and tablets travel through the stomach and blood to the whole body. Long use of steroid tablets or syrups can affect bones, eyes, growth and other organs. An inhaler gives most of the medicine where it is needed, so much less reaches the rest of the body.

Syrup kyon nahi?

Syrup aur tablet pait aur blood ke raste poore sharir mein jaate hain. Lambe samay tak steroid tablet ya syrup lene se haddiyon, aankhon, growth aur doosre organs par asar ho sakta hai. Inhaler zyadatar dawa wahin pahunchata hai jahan zaroorat hai, isliye sharir ke baaki hisson tak bahut kam jaata hai.

8

Is the inhaler medicine the same as the nebuliser medicine?

Yes. Both use the same medicine, budesonide (Budecort). The difference is the dose. A nebuliser treatment is 0.5 mg (500 mcg), which is five times one inhaler puff of 100 mcg. Both stay mostly in the airways, and very little reaches the blood. The doctor may choose the nebuliser for very young children, a severe attack, or when a child cannot yet use an inhaler well.

Inhaler aur nebuliser ki dawa same hai?

Haan. Dono mein wahi dawa hai, budesonide (Budecort). Farak sirf dose ka hai. Nebuliser treatment mein 0.5 mg (500 mcg) hota hai, jo ek inhaler puff (100 mcg) se paanch guna hai. Dono hi zyadatar airways mein rehte hain, aur blood mein bahut kam jaate hain. Doctor bahut chhote bachchon, gambhir attack, ya jab bachcha inhaler theek se na le paaye, tab nebuliser suggest kar sakte hain.

9

Will the steroid make my child addicted?

No. Children do not become dependent on it or crave it. It works inside the airways to reduce swelling, not in a way that creates a habit.

Kya steroid se bachche ko aadat padegi?

Nahi. Bachche isper nirbhar nahi hote aur isse nashe jaisi chahat nahi hoti. Yeh airways ke andar sooj kam karta hai.

10

Will it harm my child's growth or weaken their body?

At the doses prescribed for inhalers, the effect on growth is very small. Your doctor checks height and weight at visits. Low-dose inhaled steroids do not stop most children from fighting infection. Keep vaccines up to date, and call the doctor for fever or a chest infection.

Kya isse bachche ki growth ya immunity kam hogi?

Inhaler ki prescribed dose par growth par asar bahut kam hota hai. Doctor har visit par height aur weight check karte hain. Kam dose wale inhaled steroid zyadatar bachchon ki infection se ladne ki takat nahi rokte. Vaccine time par lagwayein, aur bukhaar ya chest infection par doctor ko dikhayein.

11

Can I stop the medicine when my child feels well?

Please do not stop or change it on your own. Stopping suddenly can bring the cough and wheeze back. Talk to your doctor first. The doctor will reduce the treatment slowly after a long period of good health.

Bachcha theek lage toh dawa band kar sakte hain?

Kripya apne aap dawa band ya badal mat dijiye. Achanak band karne se khaansi aur wheeze wapas aa sakte hain. Pehle doctor se baat karein. Lambe samay tak theek rehne par doctor dheere-dheere dawa kam karenge.

12

My child cries and refuses the inhaler. What can I do?

  • Practise when your child is calm, not during a cough.
  • Let your child hold the device, and use a favourite story or song.
  • Praise them afterwards. A mask suits younger children; a mouthpiece suits children who can close their lips around it.
  • Ask the doctor to show you the technique again. It is normal to need a few tries.

Bachcha ro raha hai aur inhaler se mana kar raha hai. Kya karein?

  • Jab bachcha shant ho, tab practice karein, khaansi ke time nahi.
  • Device pakadne dein, aur koi pasandida kahani ya gaana sunayein.
  • Baad mein shabashi dein. Chhote bachchon ke liye mask, aur jo honth band kar sakte hain unke liye mouthpiece.
  • Doctor se technique dobara dikhane ko kahein. Kuch baar koshish karna normal hai.
13

Does my child need antibiotics for a cold?

No. Colds are caused by viruses, and antibiotics do not work on viruses. Antibiotics are used only when the doctor finds a bacterial infection.

Cold ke liye antibiotic chahiye?

Nahi. Cold virus se hota hai, aur antibiotic virus par kaam nahi karti. Antibiotic tabhi di jaati hai jab doctor ko bacterial infection mile.

14

My family does not agree to an inhaler. What can be given instead?

An inhaler is usually the first choice, because it works best with the fewest side effects. If the family does not agree, the doctor may suggest a tablet called montelukast. It works differently from an inhaler:

  • For most children with repeated wheeze, it is much less effective than an inhaler. It may not control the cough and wheeze as well.
  • It goes into the blood and through the whole body, so it may cause more side effects than an inhaler. The inhaler medicine mostly stays in the airways.
  • Rarely, sleep or mood changes can happen. If you notice them, tell the doctor.
  • Do not start, stop or change any tablet on your own. Dose as prescribed by your doctor.

Your family's worries are important. Ask the doctor to explain the options to everyone together, and write your questions down before the visit.

Mere ghar wale inhaler ke liye raazi nahi hain. Phir kya diya ja sakta hai?

Inhaler aam taur par pehli pasand hota hai, kyonki yeh sabse achha kaam karta hai aur side effects sabse kam hote hain. Agar ghar wale raazi nahi hain, toh doctor montelukast naam ki tablet suggest kar sakte hain. Yeh inhaler se alag tarah kaam karti hai:

  • Baar baar aane wale wheeze wale zyadatar bachchon mein yeh inhaler se kaafi kam asardar hai. Khaansi aur wheeze utni achhi tarah kaabu mein na aayein.
  • Yeh blood mein jaakar poore sharir mein jaati hai, isliye inhaler ke mukable side effects zyada ho sakte hain. Inhaler ki dawa zyadatar airways mein hi rehti hai.
  • Kabhi kabhi neend ya mood mein badlaav ho sakta hai. Aisa dikhe toh doctor ko batayein.
  • Apne aap koi tablet shuru, band ya badal na karein. Dose doctor ke prescription ke anusaar.

Parivaar ki chinta jaayaz hai. Doctor se sab ke saath baithkar options samjhne ko kahein, aur visit se pehle apne sawaal likh lein.

Dose as prescribed by your doctor.

Follow the number of puffs and the timing on your child's prescription. Ask the doctor if anything is unclear.

Dose doctor ke prescription ke anusaar. Prescription par likhi puffs aur timing ke anusaar hi dein.

English: Feeling better does not always mean the airway has healed. Regular treatment and check-ups protect your child's airways between colds.

Hinglish: Bachcha theek dikhne ka matlab hamesha yeh nahi ki airway theek ho gaya. Niyamit dawa aur check-up cold ke beech bhi airways ko bachaate hain.
