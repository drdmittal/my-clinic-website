Master Guide: Is Your Child Not Eating Well or a Selective/Fussy Eater?

Dealing with selective eating is a normal developmental phase starting around 1 year of age. Here is standard educational guidance for parents:

1. Loving & Structured Boundaries

Never become a short-order cook. Serve the family meal neutrally: "You don't have to eat, but this is what is on the table." Remove plate after 20 mins without anger.

2. Neutral Repeated Exposure

It takes up to 15 exposures for a child's brain to accept a new food. Simply having it on their plate and seeing you eat it breaks down food phobia.

3. De-escalate Meal Pressure

Anxiety kills appetite. Never nag or beg ("Just one more bite!"). Keep dinner table conversations fun, light, and completely unrelated to eating.

4. Eliminate Liquid Calorie Traps

Children may fill up on milk or juices all day. Cap milk to age limits (e.g., max 400ml/day for toddlers) and offer water between meals.
