🤱 0–6 Months — Breastfeeding

🤱 0–6 महीने — Breastfeeding

For most healthy term babies, exclusive breastfeeding is recommended for the first 6 months, with breastfeeding started as early as possible after birth and continued alongside complementary foods later.

अधिकांश healthy term babies में पहले 6 महीने exclusive breastfeeding की सलाह दी जाती है। बाद में complementary food शुरू होने के साथ breastfeeding जारी रखी जा सकती है।

Exclusive breastfeeding
Breast milk only — no routine water, honey, ghutti or other feeds unless medically indicated. Breast milk ही feed — routine पानी, शहद, घुट्टी या दूसरा feed नहीं, जब तक medical indication न हो।

Early Hunger Cues / शुरुआती Hunger Cues

  • Stirring or waking — जागना या हिलना
  • Hands toward mouth — हाथ मुँह की ओर ले जाना
  • Rooting/searching movements — breast खोजने जैसी movement
  • Sucking movements — चूसने जैसी movement

How Do I Know Milk Is Enough?

Do not judge milk supply only by crying. Look at effective feeding, swallowing, urine output, hydration and growth.

सिर्फ रोने से low milk तय न करें। Feeding effectiveness, swallowing, urine output, hydration और growth देखें।

My Baby Is Not Latching / Baby Latch नहीं कर रहा

Possible reasons: positioning, shallow attachment, engorgement, sleepiness, prematurity or another issue needing assessment.

क्या कारण हो सकता है: positioning, shallow latch, breast fullness, sleepy baby, prematurity या कोई अन्य issue।

Try: bring baby close, tummy toward mother and allow a deep mouthful of breast rather than nipple-only attachment.

Try करें: Baby को close रखें, tummy mother की तरफ हो और सिर्फ nipple नहीं बल्कि breast का अच्छा हिस्सा mouth में आए।

Seek help if: feeds remain painful, nipples repeatedly crack, baby cannot maintain attachment or weight gain is poor.

My Nipples Hurt / Nipples में दर्द

Persistent or severe pain should not simply be accepted as normal. Check latch and positioning.

Persistent या severe pain को “normal” मानकर ignore न करें। Latch और positioning check कराएँ।

I Think My Milk Is Low / मुझे लगता है Milk कम है

Frequent feeding, cluster feeding, softer breasts or crying do not automatically prove low supply. Assess growth, feeding technique, swallowing and urine output.

Frequent feeding, cluster feeding, breasts का soft लगना या baby का रोना अपने आप low supply साबित नहीं करता। Growth, latch, swallowing और urine output देखें।

Breast Fullness / Engorgement

Frequent effective feeding may help. If there is significant redness, pain, fever or maternal illness, seek medical assessment.

Frequent effective feeding helpful हो सकती है। ज्यादा redness, pain, fever या mother unwell हो तो medical assessment कराएँ।

Breastfeeding Myths

“Thin milk is weak.” ❌ False
“Colostrum should be discarded.” ❌ False
“Baby needs water in summer.” ❌ Not routinely in an exclusively breastfed baby under 6 months
“Small breasts make less milk.” ❌ False

🥣 6–8 Months — Starting Complementary Feeding

🥣 6–8 महीने — Complementary Feeding शुरू करें

Food starts. Breastfeeding continues.
खाना शुरू करें। Breastfeeding बंद नहीं।

Start with small amounts and gradually increase quantity, variety and texture. Prefer thick mashed or semi-solid foods rather than relying only on watery dal water or rice water.

छोटी मात्रा से शुरू करें और धीरे-धीरे quantity, variety और texture बढ़ाएँ। सिर्फ पतली दाल का पानी या चावल का पानी देने की बजाय thick mashed/semi-solid food दें।

Texture tip: Food should be thick enough to stay on the spoon.
Food spoon पर टिकने जितना thick रखें — सिर्फ flavoured water जैसा नहीं।

Recipes / Recipes

🥬 Moong Dal Khichdi Mash

Rice + yellow moong dal + water. Cook very soft. Mash to a spoon-thick texture. A small amount of ghee/oil may be added if appropriate.

Rice + yellow moong dal बहुत soft पकाएँ। Spoon-thick mash करें।

🥬 Ragi Porridge

Cook ragi flour properly in water. Keep it thick and spoonable rather than watery. No added sugar is needed.

🥬 Curd Rice Mash

Very soft rice + plain curd. Mash and serve fresh.

🥬 Mashed Banana

Use ripe banana. Mash with a fork. No added sugar required.

Jain note: Recipes avoid onion, garlic and commonly avoided root vegetables. Please additionally exclude foods your family personally avoids.
🌿 Jain Moong-Rice Khichdi

Rice + moong dal. Cook very soft. Add only family-approved vegetables if desired.

🌿 Jain Ragi Porridge

Ragi + water. Cook properly and keep spoon-thick.

🌿 Curd Rice Mash

Soft rice + plain curd.

🌿 Banana / Papaya Mash

Use soft ripe fruit. Mash with a fork.

🥚 Soft Egg Mash

Use thoroughly cooked egg. Mash finely. It may be mixed with soft rice or khichdi as the child accepts texture.

🥚 Egg Khichdi

Soft moong-rice khichdi + well-cooked mashed egg.

🍗 Chicken Rice Mash

Cook boneless chicken thoroughly with rice. Remove all bone and cartilage. Mash into a soft age-appropriate texture.

🐟 Fish Rice Mash

Use thoroughly cooked fish. Inspect extremely carefully for bones before mixing with soft rice.

👐 8–9 Months — Texture & Finger Foods

Progress from smooth mash toward thicker mash, soft lumps and developmentally appropriate finger foods. Do not keep a normally developing baby on smooth purees indefinitely.

Smooth mash से धीरे-धीरे thicker mash, soft lumps और age-appropriate finger foods की ओर बढ़ें।

Texture ladder: Thick mash → soft lumps → safe finger foods → modified family food
🥬 Soft Vegetable Khichdi

🥬 Soft Idli Pieces

🥚 Soft Egg Pieces

🍗 Soft Chicken Khichdi

इस उम्र में appetite variation, refusal और control की इच्छा common हो सकती है। Constant grazing की जगह meal/snack routine रखें।

🚀 10–14 Years — Puberty Nutrition

Pay attention to protein, iron, calcium, vitamin D, breakfast habits, body image and overall dietary quality.

Protein, iron, calcium, vitamin D, breakfast habits, body image और overall diet quality पर ध्यान दें।

Protein Dal, paneer, curd, soy, egg, chicken or fish depending on diet.
Iron Pulses, legumes, leafy vegetables, egg/meat where consumed.
Calcium Milk, curd, paneer or suitable alternatives.
Body image Avoid shame and extreme dieting.

Menstruating Adolescents

Iron deserves particular attention. If iron deficiency is suspected, assess rather than using random supplements.

Periods शुरू होने के बाद iron की जरूरत पर खास ध्यान दें। Iron deficiency का शक हो तो random supplement की बजाय assessment कराएँ।

💪 14–18 Years — Nutrition Independence

Discuss eating out, meal skipping, gym diets, protein supplements, body image and sensible food choices.

Eating out, meal skipping, gym diets, protein supplements, body image और sensible choices पर बात करें।

Protein powder: Most healthy adolescents do not need protein supplements simply because they joined a gym. First assess diet, training, growth and the reason for use.
Sports supplements: Do not start them simply on advice from gym friends or social-media influencers.

Eating-Disorder Warning Signs

  • Rapid or unexplained weight loss
  • Extreme fear of weight gain
  • Persistent meal skipping
  • Very restrictive diets
  • Vomiting after meals
  • Excessive exercise linked to guilt
  • Marked body-image distress

❤️ How to Feed — Not Just What to Feed

❤️ क्या खिलाएँ ही नहीं, कैसे खिलाएँ यह भी महत्वपूर्ण है

  • Respect hunger and fullness cues.
  • Encourage without force.
  • Allow age-appropriate mess and self-feeding.
  • Eat together when practical.
  • Do not use screens as the default feeding tool.
  • Offer new foods repeatedly without pressure.
“बस एक bite और!”
“खाना यहाँ है। जितनी भूख है उतना खाओ।”
“सब्ज़ी खाओ तभी dessert मिलेगा।”
“सब्ज़ी भी meal का हिस्सा है। चाहो तो taste कर सकते हो।”

📱 Screen Feeding Step-Down

  1. Reduce screen stimulation and move it farther away.
  2. Turn it off for part of one meal.
  3. Create one screen-free meal each day.
  4. Gradually increase screen-free meals.
“YouTube बाद में देखेंगे। अभी खाना साथ बैठकर खाएँगे।”

😣 Picky Eating / Selective Eating

Repeated exposure is often needed, but there is no single magic number. Keep one familiar food on the plate, offer a tiny amount of the new food, avoid pressure and model eating it yourself.

Repeated exposure helpful हो सकती है, लेकिन कोई एक magic number नहीं है। Plate पर एक familiar food रखें, new food थोड़ी मात्रा में दें, pressure न डालें और खुद खाकर model करें।

“My Child Eats Only White Foods.”

Use gradual food chaining instead of suddenly removing accepted foods.

Accepted foods अचानक हटाने की बजाय gradual food chaining करें।

White bread ↓ lightly toasted bread ↓ paneer sandwich ↓ paneer + tiny vegetable ↓ vegetable-paneer sandwich
Seek assessment if: diet is extremely restricted, there is gagging/swallowing difficulty, poor growth, nutritional deficiency or strong sensory/feeding concerns.

🌱 Vegetarian & Jain Nutrition

Vegetarian diets can be nutritionally adequate when varied. Pay attention to protein, iron, vitamin B12, calcium, vitamin D, zinc and omega-3 sources.

Vegetarian diet nutritionally adequate हो सकती है, लेकिन variety जरूरी है। Protein, iron, B12, calcium, vitamin D, zinc और omega-3 पर ध्यान दें।

Protein Dal, rajma, chana, paneer, curd, soy, nuts/seeds in age-safe forms.
Iron Pulses, legumes, leafy vegetables + vitamin-C-rich foods.
Vitamin B12 Especially important if animal-derived foods are absent or minimal.
Jain families Restrictions vary; adapt recipes to household practice.

🥜 Food Allergy & Choking Safety

Allergy Is Not the Same as Choking

Food allergy Immune reaction to a food.
Choking Mechanical blockage of the airway.

Common Choking Hazards

  • Whole nuts
  • Whole grapes
  • Popcorn
  • Hard candy
  • Hard raw vegetable pieces
  • Large chunks of food

Gagging vs Choking

Gagging Child can usually cough, make noise and breathe.
Choking May involve ineffective/no cough, inability to cry or speak, breathing difficulty or colour change.
Emergency: Suspected choking with breathing difficulty, severe allergic reaction or another acute medical emergency requires urgent local emergency care. This webpage does not replace proper pediatric first-aid training.

🚫 Foods to Avoid or Limit

Item Guidance
Honey Avoid under 12 months.
Whole nuts Avoid in young children because of choking risk. Use safe forms when appropriate.
Added sugar Keep low. Avoid making sweets a routine reward.
Sugary drinks / juice Limit. Water and whole fruit are generally preferable.
Energy drinks Not a routine hydration drink for children or adolescents.
Ghutti / unproven herbal feeds Avoid routine use in infants.
Unproven appetite stimulants Do not use without medical advice.

❓ Common Parent Questions

My 8-month-old has no teeth. Can I give solids?

Yes. Complementary feeding does not need to wait for visible teeth. Texture should match developmental ability.

हाँ। दाँत आने का इंतजार जरूरी नहीं है। Food की texture बच्चे की developmental ability के अनुसार रखें।

My Child Drinks Too Much Milk. What Should I Do?

Create milk times rather than offering milk whenever the child refuses food. If growth is poor, do not make a major change without assessment.

My Toddler Takes One Hour to Eat.

Review grazing, milk timing, screen feeding, meal routine and feeding pressure. Meals should not become endless negotiations.

My Child Only Eats While Watching YouTube.

Use the gradual screen step-down plan rather than turning every meal into a fight.

Does My Child Need Protein Powder?

Usually not simply because the child is active or goes to the gym. First assess diet, growth, training and actual need.

Can I Give Nuts?

Whole nuts are a choking hazard in young children. Nut products may sometimes be offered in safe age-appropriate forms such as smooth paste/powder depending on allergy considerations and individual circumstances.

Can I Give Fruit Juice?

Whole fruit is generally preferable because it provides fibre and encourages normal eating skills. Avoid making juice a routine replacement for water or fruit.

My Child Is Thin but Very Active. Should I Worry?

Appearance alone cannot diagnose undernutrition. Look at the child's height, weight and growth trajectory on an appropriate growth chart.

🚩 Growth & Nutrition Red Flags

  • Poor weight gain or downward crossing of growth centiles
  • Persistent weight loss
  • Severe feeding refusal
  • Swallowing difficulty or recurrent choking
  • Persistent vomiting
  • Chronic diarrhoea or blood in stool
  • Significant abdominal distension
  • Suspected micronutrient deficiency
  • Severe constipation
  • Suspected food allergy
  • Developmental feeding difficulty
  • Eating-disorder concerns in adolescents
Urgent help: acute choking, significant breathing difficulty, severe allergic reaction, marked dehydration, severe lethargy or another medical emergency.

Need Individual Nutrition Advice?

Individual Nutrition Advice चाहिए?

Children with poor growth, prematurity, allergies, obesity, chronic disease, developmental feeding problems or very restricted diets may need individualized assessment.

Poor growth, prematurity, allergy, obesity, chronic disease, developmental feeding problems या बहुत restricted diet में individualized assessment की जरूरत हो सकती है।
