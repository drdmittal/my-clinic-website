Doctor's shelf (private)

Links to share with specific parents. Not linked from the website.

Hidden, not locked. Anyone who has this exact address can open it. Do not post it or put it on the menu, and do not share it in public. Share only the individual links.
To add a link: open shelf.html on GitHub, edit the SHELF list at the bottom of this file, copy one block as a template, fill in title, type, URL, note and topics, then commit. The page updates in 2 to 3 minutes.

Types: Article, Video, Tool, Other. Topics are short words used for search (for example fever, newborn).
